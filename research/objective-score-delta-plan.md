# Objective score delta test (research only)

The original 61-plant/eight-disable cohort is discovery data. The separate 12-map occurrence extension (32 plants, 12 disables) has not been used to choose this score rule. Public actor labels will be attached only after replay-derived candidates are frozen.

Observed score counters in cached discovery rounds support +75 as the common isolated assist change (94 examples), while isolated kill-counter changes have several common score changes: +100 (117), +120 (123), +110 (33), +130 (24), and larger batched values. These are score observations, not universal scoring constants. Some assist-associated changes are +175 and +95. A fixed subtraction of +100 for every kill is therefore unsafe.

The 4139 R07 replay is a decisive conflict: five SSG players have stable scoreboard identity bindings. After the plant-state packet, Aiden gets +100 at +852 bytes, Raid gets +10 at +2,425, then a Raid kill counter increments at +13,955 and the later team score wave includes Raid +200 and four teammates +100. A simple +100 score pick incorrectly names Aiden. Subtracting an assumed +100 kill from Raid still leaves Aiden as the apparent +100 residual. The known public actor Raid is validation evidence only.

Freeze a deliberately conservative **diagnostic**, not a production actor resolver: require complete one-to-one scoreboard identity binding for the eligible side; look for exactly one eligible player with an exact +100 score update within 1,500 decompressed bytes after the validated objective-state packet; abstain if any kill/assist counter increments from 5,000 bytes before through 20,000 bytes after that packet. The 1,500-byte candidate interval covers the observed close score packets in discovery, including a +1,373 example. The broader collision check spans the later score wave observed in 4139 R07. These windows are provisional packet-distance descriptions, not game-time guarantees. Discovery replay labels showed 25 correct plants and one correct disable under this abstaining rule; that number is in-sample and may not generalize.

The unchanged rule was applied once to the 12-map extension: plants **8 correct / 0 incorrect / 24 unresolved**; disables **0 correct / 0 incorrect / 12 unresolved**. The extension is now **consumed** for actor research, and any changed rule needs a new independent set. A zero-error small extension does not establish that +100 uniquely represents objective credit across builds; retain unresolved actors in live data until stronger independent evidence exists. See `research/output/objective-score-batch-comparison.md` for packet-level post-validation diagnosis.
